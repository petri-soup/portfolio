I’m a 3D artist, game developer, and painter using game engines to populate digital ecosystems with speculative flora and fauna. My work operates on two timescales: looking backward, virtual fossil fields preserve evolutionary history and extinct organisms; Looking forward, speculative environments rehearse near futures of climate resilience.

I'm a research assistant at the DWELL Lab

Recent work has been exhibited at Art Basel Miami, the Microsoft Garage, Lima Film Festival, the New Museum, and the web publication Tractor Beam. I've held fellowships from the Maharam Foundation, Agog, NASA, and the National Science Foundation, and completed residencies at Haystack Mountain School of Craft and Fish Factory Creative Center in Iceland.
